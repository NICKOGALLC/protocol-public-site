Protocol Personal Health brings dose history, measurements, lab reports, and wearable summaries together in a private tracker.

This public site describes the application and its data practices. The tracker is a separate, privately operated installation; this site does not provide access to health records or connect to a Google account.

Lab reports

Wearable summaries

Use read-only Google Health access to collect available activity, health measurements, and sleep records. View daily, weekly, and monthly trends, compact heart-rate curves, and session summaries.

04

Workout controls

An optional paired Android companion can display shared Bluetooth heart rate and log personal workout sessions. It does not remotely start a wearable’s internal workout mode.

Read-only connection.
Owner-controlled storage.

When the owner authorizes Google Health, Protocol reads the permitted data into its self-hosted installation for personal charts and analysis. It does not write, edit, or delete Google Health records.

Long-term wearable history uses daily summaries, 15-minute heart-rate curves, and compact sleep and exercise sessions. These displays describe available data and timing; they do not establish medical diagnoses or cause and effect.

How Google Health data is used ↗

Protocol is a personal tracking application. It is not an emergency monitoring service or a substitute for professional medical care. Protocol is independent of Google and Fitbit.
